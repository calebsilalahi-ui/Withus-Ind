Our story

Cerita kami

WithUs Indonesia is operated by PT Harpa Ananta Sora, led by a team with more than 20 years of industry experience. We work across artist management, IP development and live event production, building a community where art meets industry.

WithUs Indonesia dikelola oleh PT Harpa Ananta Sora, dipimpin tim dengan pengalaman lebih dari 20 tahun di industri. Kami berkarya di manajemen artis, pengembangan IP, dan produksi event langsung, membangun komunitas tempat seni bertemu dengan industri.
