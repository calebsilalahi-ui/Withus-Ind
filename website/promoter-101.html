Akuisisi Artis & Penawaran

02

Contract & Deposit

Kontrak & Deposit

Negotiate and sign the performance agreement. Fee structures vary: a flat guarantee, a versus deal (the higher of guarantee or a share of ticket revenue) or a guarantee plus a backend share. A deposit, a portion of the guarantee, is paid upfront and is usually non-refundable if the date is cancelled.

Menegosiasikan dan menandatangani kontrak. Struktur fee bervariasi: guarantee tetap, versus deal (yang lebih tinggi antara guarantee atau porsi pendapatan tiket), atau guarantee plus backend. Deposit, sebagian dari guarantee, dibayar di muka dan umumnya tidak dikembalikan bila tanggal dibatalkan.

Who is involvedSiapa terlibat
Promoter, legal, agency, financePromotor, legal, agensi, keuangan
Key riskRisiko utama
Unfavourable terms, deposit exposure, cancellation liability.Klausul tidak seimbang, eksposur deposit, tanggung jawab pembatalan.
03

Venue & Permits

Venue & Perizinan

Secure the venue, the police crowd permit (izin keramaian) and the location permit. Applications go through the digital licensing system and must be filed well ahead; permits are not issued a day before the show.

Mengamankan venue, izin keramaian dari Polri, dan izin lokasi. Pengajuan melalui sistem perizinan digital dan harus jauh hari; izin tidak diterbitkan sehari sebelum acara.

Who is involvedSiapa terlibat
Promoter, venue, authoritiesPromotor, venue, otoritas
Key riskRisiko utama
Date conflicts and late or denied permits.Bentrok tanggal serta izin yang terlambat atau ditolak.
04

Budget & P&L

Anggaran & P&L

Build the event budget and a profit-and-loss model across scenarios. Local entertainment tax (PBJT) and music performance royalties are budget lines, not afterthoughts.

Menyusun anggaran event dan model laba-rugi untuk berbagai skenario. Pajak hiburan daerah (PBJT) dan royalti hak pertunjukan musik adalah pos anggaran sejak awal.

Menetapkan kategori dan alokasi, memilih platform, menjalankan on-sale, dan rekonsiliasi. Biaya layanan platform umumnya dibagi antara venue, platform, dan promotor, tergantung kontrak.

Concert Financial Architecture & Rules in Indonesia

Arsitektur Finansial Konser & Regulasi di Indonesia

Every major concert in Indonesia operates across five fundamental cost pillars and strict statutory settlement obligations.

Setiap konser besar di Indonesia beroperasi di atas lima pilar biaya utama dan kewajiban settlement regulasi yang ketat.

Production / Artist Fee Produksi / Fee Artis

Artist guarantee, staging technical riders, international flights, and VIP hospitality. Guarantee artis, rider teknis panggung, penerbangan internasional, dan hospitality VIP.

Venue Rental Sewa Venue

Arena/stadium lease, structural load calculation, electrical gensets, and build-out days. Sewa arena/stadion, kalkulasi beban struktur, genset kelistrikan, dan hari load-in.

Taxes & Permits Pajak & Perizinan

10% PBJT local entertainment tax, LMKN music royalties, police crowd permits, and C7A visas. Pajak hiburan PBJT 10%, royalti musik LMKN, izin keramaian Polri, dan visa artis C7A.

Marketing / Operations Marketing / Operasional

National digital campaigns, media press days, certified crowd security, and on-site paramedics. Kampanye digital nasional, konferensi pers, pengamanan bersertifikat, dan tim medis.

Ticketing Fees Biaya Tiket

Primary ticketing platform service fees, turnstile scanning, RFID wristbands, and payment gateways. Biaya layanan platform tiket primer, pemindaian barcode, gelang RFID, dan payment gateway.

Deal Glossary & Contract Standards

Glosarium Kontrak & Standar Kesepakatan

Essential commercial deal structures governing live touring and artist representation in Indonesia.

Struktur kesepakatan komersial utama yang mengatur tur konser dan manajemen artis di Indonesia.

Guarantee

Fixed Fee

Fixed fee paid to the artist regardless of ticket attendance. The financial and ticket sales risk sits entirely with the promoter.

Fee tetap yang dibayarkan kepada artis berapa pun jumlah tiket terjual. Risiko finansial dan penjualan tiket sepenuhnya ditanggung promotor.

Versus Deal

Hybrid Rate

The artist receives the higher of the agreed guarantee or a pre-negotiated percentage of gross ticket receipts.

Artis menerima nilai yang lebih tinggi antara guarantee atau persentase tertentu dari total pendapatan tiket kotor.

Backend

Revenue Share

A share of net revenue paid on top of the guarantee once the show surpasses its contractual break-even point.

Porsi pendapatan bersih yang dibayarkan di atas guarantee setelah konser melampaui titik impas (break-even point) yang disepakati.

Deposit

Advance

A substantial percentage of the guarantee paid upon contract signing, typically non-refundable if the promoter cancels.

Persentase pembayaran fee artis yang disetor di muka saat kontrak diteken, umumnya tidak dapat dikembalikan jika promotor membatalkan acara.

Settlement

Reconciliation

Final reconciliation of certified ticket revenue, statutory taxes, platform fees, and production costs into the concluding payout.

Rekonsiliasi akhir dari penerimaan tiket resmi, pajak daerah, biaya platform, dan biaya produksi menjadi pembayaran akhir.

Promoter FAQ

Pertanyaan Seputar Promotor

Who pays the artist and finances production costs? Siapa yang membayar artis dan membiayai biaya produksi? +

Under standard entertainment agreements, the local promoter pays the artist fee and bears all local venue, production, and tax liabilities, typically structured with a 50% advance deposit upon contract execution and remaining balance at D-0.

Berdasarkan kontrak pertunjukan standar, promotor lokal menanggung fee artis serta seluruh kewajiban sewa venue, produksi, dan pajak. Pembayaran umumnya berstruktur 50% deposit di muka saat kontrak diteken dan pelunasan saat hari-H (D-0).

Why do live shows get cancelled, rescheduled, or adjusted? Mengapa pertunjukan konser bisa dibatalkan atau dijadwalkan ulang? +

Primary causes include statutory permit delays, artist routing conflicts, structural venue safety, or force majeure events. In any event adjustment, verified ticket refund procedures are managed strictly through the authorized ticketing platform.

Penyebab utama meliputi keterlambatan perizinan resmi, bentrok jadwal tur internasional, keselamatan struktur panggung, atau force majeure. Jika terjadi perubahan, pengembalian dana dikelola langsung oleh platform tiket resmi berlisensi.

Where can audiences purchase genuine official tickets? Di mana penonton dapat membeli tiket konser resmi? +

Exclusively via the direct official ticketing URLs linked in our production portfolio. WithUs Indonesia does not accept direct public retail payments on this corporate portal to protect fans from fraudulent secondary market resale.

Hanya melalui tautan platform tiket resmi berlisensi yang tertera di portofolio kami. WithUs Indonesia tidak menerima transaksi tiket ritel secara langsung di situs ini demi melindungi penonton dari penipuan calo sekunder.
