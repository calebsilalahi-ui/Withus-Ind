What does a promoter actually do?

Apa sebenarnya yang dikerjakan promotor?

Ten steps behind every concert. Scroll to follow the cycle.

Sepuluh langkah di balik setiap konser. Gulir untuk mengikuti siklusnya.

01

Artist Acquisition & Offer

Akuisisi Artis & Penawaran

Identify the artist, test demand and send a formal offer to the artist's agency.

Mengidentifikasi artis, menguji permintaan, dan mengirim penawaran resmi ke agensi artis.

Who is involvedSiapa terlibat
Promoter, artist agency / principalPromotor, agensi / principal artis
Key riskRisiko utama
Overpaying for a fee the market cannot absorb.Membayar fee yang tidak mampu diserap pasar.
02

Contract & Deposit

Kontrak & Deposit

Negotiate and sign the performance agreement. Fee structures vary: a flat guarantee, a versus deal (the higher of guarantee or a share of ticket revenue) or a guarantee plus a backend share. A deposit, a portion of the guarantee, is paid upfront and is usually non-refundable if the date is cancelled.

Menegosiasikan dan menandatangani kontrak. Struktur fee bervariasi: guarantee tetap, versus deal (yang lebih tinggi antara guarantee atau porsi pendapatan tiket), atau guarantee plus backend. Deposit, sebagian dari guarantee, dibayar di muka dan umumnya tidak dikembalikan bila tanggal dibatalkan.

Who is involvedSiapa terlibat
Promoter, legal, agency, financePromotor, legal, agensi, keuangan
Key riskRisiko utama
Unfavourable terms, deposit exposure, cancellation liability.Klausul tidak seimbang, eksposur deposit, tanggung jawab pembatalan.
03

Venue & Permits

Venue & Perizinan

Secure the venue, the police crowd permit (izin keramaian) and the location permit. Applications go through the digital licensing system and must be filed well ahead; permits are not issued a day before the show.

Mengamankan venue, izin keramaian dari Polri, dan izin lokasi. Pengajuan melalui sistem perizinan digital dan harus jauh hari; izin tidak diterbitkan sehari sebelum acara.

Who is involvedSiapa terlibat
Promoter, venue, authoritiesPromotor, venue, otoritas
Key riskRisiko utama
Date conflicts and late or denied permits.Bentrok tanggal serta izin yang terlambat atau ditolak.
04

Budget & P&L

Anggaran & P&L

Build the event budget and a profit-and-loss model across scenarios. Local entertainment tax (PBJT) and music performance royalties are budget lines, not afterthoughts.

Menyusun anggaran event dan model laba-rugi untuk berbagai skenario. Pajak hiburan daerah (PBJT) dan royalti hak pertunjukan musik adalah pos anggaran sejak awal.

Who is involvedSiapa terlibat
Promoter, financePromotor, keuangan
Key riskRisiko utama
Underestimated costs; break-even set too high.Biaya diremehkan; titik impas terlalu tinggi.
05

Ticketing

Tiket

Set categories and allocation, choose the platform, run the on-sale, reconcile. Platform service fees are usually shared between venue, platform and promoter, depending on the contract.

Menetapkan kategori dan alokasi, memilih platform, menjalankan on-sale, dan rekonsiliasi. Biaya layanan platform umumnya dibagi antara venue, platform, dan promotor, tergantung kontrak.

Who is involvedSiapa terlibat
Promoter, ticketing platform, financePromotor, platform tiket, keuangan
Key riskRisiko utama
Slow sales, fraud and reselling, settlement delays.Penjualan lambat, penipuan dan calo, keterlambatan settlement.

Produksi & Logistik

Panggung, suara, pencahayaan, rider, perjalanan, hospitality, dan rencana keamanan.

Run-of-show, command structure, crowd flow and escalation paths.

Run-of-show, struktur komando, alur penonton, dan jalur eskalasi.

10

Settlement & Report

Settlement & Laporan

Reconcile ticket revenue, taxes, fees, artist and promoter costs and other income into a final payout. With a deposit paid, settlement is the remaining balance plus any backend share.

Merekonsiliasi pendapatan tiket, pajak, biaya, ongkos artis dan promotor, serta pendapatan lain menjadi pembayaran akhir. Bila deposit sudah dibayar, settlement adalah sisa pembayaran ditambah porsi backend jika ada.

Promoter, finance, agency, platformPromotor, keuangan, agensi, platform
Key riskRisiko utama
Disputes over figures; slow reconciliation.Sengketa angka; rekonsiliasi lambat.

The rules of the game in Indonesia

Aturan main di Indonesia

What a promoter must handle before a foreign artist can take the stage. Regulations change, so confirm the current text with the authority before relying on it. This is education, not legal advice.

Hal yang harus diurus promotor sebelum artis asing naik panggung. Regulasi berubah, jadi pastikan teks terbaru ke instansi terkait sebelum dijadikan acuan. Ini edukasi, bukan nasihat hukum.

Crowd permit and location permit

Izin keramaian dan izin lokasi

Concerts need a police crowd permit (izin keramaian), filed at least a week ahead according to police guidance, plus a location permit from the venue owner or local government. Online licensing is now used for international events.

Konser memerlukan izin keramaian dari Polri, diajukan minimal seminggu sebelumnya menurut panduan kepolisian, plus izin lokasi dari pemilik venue atau pemerintah daerah. Perizinan daring kini dipakai untuk event internasional.

Entertainment tax (PBJT)

Pajak hiburan (PBJT)

In Jakarta, concerts fall under PBJT on arts and entertainment services at 10 percent of the ticket price under Regional Regulation 1/2024. The 40 percent rate applies to nightclubs, karaoke, bars and spas, not concerts.

Di Jakarta, konser termasuk PBJT jasa kesenian dan hiburan sebesar 10 persen dari harga tiket menurut Perda 1/2024. Tarif 40 persen berlaku untuk diskotek, karaoke, kelab malam, bar, dan spa, bukan konser.

Performance royalties

Royalti hak pertunjukan

Under Indonesian practice, the organiser or venue owner, not the performer, is responsible for paying live performance royalties for music.

Dalam praktik di Indonesia, penyelenggara atau pemilik venue, bukan performer, bertanggung jawab membayar royalti pertunjukan musik secara langsung.

Visas for foreign artists

Visa untuk artis asing

Performers need a performer visa (C7A) sponsored by the Indonesian organiser and filed before travel; support crew need a separate category (C7B). A tourist visa does not cover a paid performance. Work permit paperwork was simplified for short music engagements.

Performer membutuhkan visa pertunjukan (C7A) yang disponsori penyelenggara di Indonesia dan diajukan sebelum perjalanan; kru pendukung membutuhkan kategori terpisah (C7B). Visa turis tidak mencakup pertunjukan berbayar. Dokumen izin kerja disederhanakan untuk penampilan musik jangka pendek.

Ticketing fees

Biaya tiket

Venues often sign with one primary ticketing platform. Service fees on top of the face value are typically shared between the venue, the platform and the promoter, and splits differ by contract.

Venue sering terikat dengan satu platform tiket primer. Biaya layanan di atas harga tiket umumnya dibagi antara venue, platform, dan promotor, dengan porsi berbeda tiap kontrak.

GuaranteeFixed fee paid to the artist whatever the attendance; the risk sits with the promoter.Fee tetap untuk artis berapa pun jumlah penonton; risikonya ada pada promotor.
Versus dealThe artist receives the higher of the guarantee or a percentage of ticket revenue.Artis menerima yang lebih tinggi antara guarantee atau persentase pendapatan tiket.
BackendA share of revenue above an agreed break-point, paid on top of the guarantee.Porsi pendapatan di atas titik impas yang disepakati, dibayarkan di atas guarantee.
DepositPart of the guarantee paid upfront, commonly non-refundable if the promoter cancels.Sebagian guarantee yang dibayar di muka, umumnya tidak dikembalikan bila promotor membatalkan.

Who pays the artist?Siapa yang membayar artis?

Under the agreement, the local promoter pays the artist's fee and covers local costs, typically with a deposit upfront.

Berdasarkan kontrak, promotor lokal membayar fee artis dan biaya lokal, umumnya dengan deposit di awal.

Why do shows get cancelled or changed?Mengapa pertunjukan bisa dibatalkan atau berubah?

Common reasons are permits, artist schedules, safety, and force majeure. Refund rules follow the official terms and the ticketing platform.

Alasan umum: perizinan, jadwal artis, keselamatan, dan force majeure. Aturan refund mengikuti syarat resmi dan platform tiket.
